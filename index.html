Reef Rescue

Our global oceans are under heavy duress. Ghost fishing nets trap defenseless marine life, and synthetic plastics contaminate precious coral habitats.

As an eco-submersible pilot, your mission is to locate underwater waste zones, clean up human debris, and restore ecological balance.

Submersible Pilot Briefing

Navigation: Use W A S D or Arrow Keys to pilot your sub.

Debris Extraction: Intercept floating white plastics to remove them from the ecosystem.

Wildlife Rescue: Locate marine animals trapped in red commercial netting and touch them to break them free.

Hazard Warning: Avoid industrial red toxic mines. Striking them damages your hull structure.
